## Why This Matters for Your Work

Your best prompts to Claude are detailed — context, data, requirements, audience. Typing all that takes 2-3 minutes. Dictating it takes 30 seconds. For anyone who gives Claude complex, context-rich instructions, voice input is a 3-5x speed multiplier on input time.

## What You'll Build

A voice-to-text tool configured to work with your terminal / Claude Code, tested with a real prompt from your workflow.

## Do This Now

1. **Choose a tool.** Three options for macOS:
   - **SuperWhisper** (superwhisper.com) — Best for power users. Runs locally, fast, configurable. ~$10/month.
   - **MacWhisper** (goodsnooze.gumroad.com/l/macwhisper) — One-time purchase, good UI. ~$30.
   - **Built-in macOS Dictation** — Free. System Settings → Keyboard → Dictation. Press Fn twice to activate. Good enough to start.

2. **Start with macOS Dictation** (free, zero setup). Go to System Settings → Keyboard → Dictation → Enable. Press `Fn Fn` (double-tap function key) to start dictating anywhere, including your terminal.

3. **Test it.** Dictate a real prompt — describe something you're working on and what you need Claude to do. Compare the dictation speed vs. typing.

4. **If you like it, upgrade later.** SuperWhisper adds: custom vocabulary, faster processing, auto-paste into active window, and better accuracy for technical terms.

## You'll Know It Worked When

You dictate a 50+ word prompt to Claude in under 15 seconds that would have taken you a minute to type. The output quality is the same or better (because you gave MORE context when speaking than you would have typed).

## What Just Happened

Voice input changes how you interact with Claude. You give more context, more nuance, more "here's what I'm really thinking" — because speaking is cheaper than typing. The bottleneck isn't Claude's output, it's the richness of your input.
